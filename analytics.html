Protocol Architecture & Monetization

Safesweep aligns blockchain utility with sustainable developer monetization. By collecting a small transaction fee on dust swaps and charging a subscription for real-time monitoring, Safesweep funds its own nodes and developers.

Transactional Sweep Tax (1.0%)

Consolidating many separate tokens into a single swap results in massive gas savings (up to 80%). To capture value, Safesweep takes a 1.0% platform fee on the total consolidated swap volume. Users still save considerable funds on gas, creating a win-win.

Mempool Premium Shield (0.1 OKB / month)

Subscribing to Premium unlocks real-time monitoring. The agent scans the mempool to dynamically frontrun honeypot tokens, blocks malicious approvals at the RPC node level, and sends instant XMTP alerts about unauthorized on-chain activities.
